How to

1 · Get a wallet

Any wallet that supports PulseChain. It holds your coins and signs payments. PulseTEXT never sees your keys.

Internet Money

2 · Use PulseChain

This show runs on PulseChain, not Ethereum. Add the chain to your wallet in one tap.

3 · Get PLS

You need a little PLS for gas, even if you tip with HEX, PLSX, or USDC. If you have none yet, start here:

  • Bridge — move assets from Ethereum (first bridge often includes a little PLS for gas)
  • PulseX — swap into PLS on PulseChain
  • ChangeNOW — cross-chain swap into PLS
  • Getting started — short PulseChain onboarding guide

Bridge PulseX ChangeNOW Scan

4 · Tip and send

  1. Name + dollar tip. Write a line, or tick No message.
  2. Send to show — wallet pops up. Host decides what airs.
  3. Revoke all if you want to clear approvals to this show.
